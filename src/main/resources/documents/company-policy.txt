Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 24 paid leave days per calendar year.

Employees can apply for casual leave and earned leave through the employee portal.

Casual leave can be used for personal requirements.

Employees should submit their leave request at least two working days before the planned leave whenever possible.

Emergency leave can be requested directly from the manager.

Unused earned leave can be carried forward according to company policy.

Employees should contact HR for questions regarding leave eligibility.
